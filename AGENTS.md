# Website Starter Instructions

Start with `docs/START-HERE.md`, then use `docs/change-map.md` to locate the
smallest relevant surface.

- Keep the public website in `apps/web`.
- Keep the structured CMS in `apps/admin`.
- Keep persistence and validation in `apps/api`.
- Keep shared contracts in `packages/contracts`.
- Do not expose layout or design tokens as CMS fields.
- Update the focused map when a route, component, contract, or editable field changes.
- Treat the content in `packages/contracts/src/starterSiteData.ts` as placeholder
  content until it has been replaced and approved for the new company.
